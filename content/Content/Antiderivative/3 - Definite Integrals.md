$\int x^2\,dx=\frac13x^3+c$ is an **indefinite** integral (has $+c$).
A **definite** integral has limits and gives a number:
$$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx=\big[F(x)\big]_a^b=F(b)-F(a)$$

## Examples
$$\int_{-1}^{4}\frac{1}{5-x}\,dx=\big[-\ln|5-x|\big]_{-1}^{4}=-\ln1+\ln6=\ln6$$

$$\int_{-1}^{2}\frac{4}{(2x+1)^3}\,dx=\big[-(2x+1)^{-2}\big]_{-1}^{2}=-\frac1{25}+1=\frac{24}{25}$$
