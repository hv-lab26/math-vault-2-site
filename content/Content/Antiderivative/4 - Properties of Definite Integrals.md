## Using the properties
**Given** $\int_1^5 f(x)\,dx=8$:
- **a)** $\int_1^5 2f(x)\,dx=2\times8=16$
- **b)** $\int_5^1 f(x)\,dx=-8$
- **c)** $\int_1^5[f(x)+2]\,dx=8+8=16$
- **d)** Given $\int_2^5 f(x)\,dx=3$: $\int_1^2 f(x)\,dx=8-3=5$

**Given** $\int_{-1}^{1} f(x)\,dx=-4$:
- **a)** $\int_1^{-1} f(x)\,dx=4$
- **b)** $\int_{-1}^{1}[2+f(x)]\,dx=4-4=0$
- **c)** $\int_{-1}^{1}2f(x)\,dx=-8$
- **d)** $\int_{-1}^{1}kf(x)\,dx=7\Rightarrow-4k=7\Rightarrow k=-\frac74$

**a)** $\int_1^3 f=2$, $\int_1^6 f=-3$
$$\int_3^6 f=\int_3^1 f+\int_1^6 f=-2-3=-5$$

**b)** $\int_0^2 f=5$, $\int_4^6 f=2$, $\int_0^6 f=7$
$$\int_2^4 f=\int_2^0 f+\int_0^6 f+\int_6^4 f=-5+7-2=0$$
