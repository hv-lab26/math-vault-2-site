This is a Venn diagram. 

**Definition**: Venn diagram uses overlapping circles to show how different sets, groups, (or events) of items compare and connect. 

### The intersection of event A and event B

**Definition**: The intersection of event A and event B is the set of elements that are common to both. It is written as:

==**P(A ∩ B)**== 

*read as "A intersection B" or "A cap B."*

(A ∩ B) can be illustrated in a Venn Diagram as below:

![[Venn Diagram - Intersection.png]]

### Union of A and B

**Definition**: Union of A and B is the set of all elements in both A and B. It is written as:

==**P(A ∪ B)**==

*read as "Union of A and B" or, "A union B."*

(A ∪ B) can be illustrated in a Venn Diagram as below:

![[Venn Diagram - Union.png]]


## Examples:

**Question 1:** In a sporting club there are 80 members.  
18 play soccer only, 25 play basketball only, 22 play both soccer and basketball, and the rest play neither sport.
I, Display the information in a Venn diagram.Then find how many people:
**a** are in the club  
**b** play basketball  
**b(ii)** play basketball only  
**c** play both sports  
**d** play neither sport  
**e** play at least one sport  
**e(ii)** play exactly one sport
II, A member is chosen at random. Find:
- P(plays soccer)
- P(plays exactly one sport)
- P(plays neither)

**Question 2:** Of the students who play a sport at this school, 30 play AFL only, 22 play soccer only, 14 play both, and 9 play neither of these two sports.
There are 90 students at the school in total.
Draw a Venn diagram that illustrates the information.
Then find:
**a** how many students play a sport  
**b** how many play AFL  
**c** how many play neither AFL nor soccer  
**d** how many play no sport at all
**f** A student from the whole school is chosen at random. Find the probability that student:
- plays AFL
- plays soccer given that the student plays a sport
- plays neither
